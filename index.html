Smart Campus Micro-Payments Presentation
Slide 1 • The Campus Challenge

Why Traditional Payments Fail on University Campuses

Thousands of students rush to canteens and print shops between 10-minute lecture breaks, causing systemic bottlenecks.

📶 Cellular Deadzones

Basement cafeterias and crowded campus Wi-Fi lead to 45+ second UPI transaction timeouts and failed OTPs.

⏳ 15-Minute Queue Delays

Students spend their entire break waiting in line to order food or wait for stationery printouts.

👁️ Zero Parental / Budget Visibility

Cash or unmonitored UPI leads to overspending without daily allowance limits or academic subsidy support.

Slide 2 • Technical Architecture

Hybrid Online-Offline Micro-Payments

A robust fault-tolerant design where transactions succeed in sub-200ms even when student phones have zero internet access.

📡 Zero-Student-Data Checkout

Dynamic HMAC-SHA256 tokens generated locally on the student ID card. No 4G/5G or cellular data needed at the counter.

⚡ Edge POS Queue Buffer

If campus Wi-Fi drops, Merchant POS buffers encrypted transactions locally and batch reconciles when online.

🏛️ Central Real-time Ledger

Instantaneous balance deductions and automated financial reconciliation with college treasury.

Slide 3 • Key Innovations

Full Lifecycle Campus Commerce Ecosystem

Connecting Students, Parents, Canteen Chefs, Stationery Operators, and University Administrators.

💳 3D Virtual Smart ID Card

Interactive 3D card with rotating cryptographic barcode, RFID/NFC emulation, and parent budget controls.

🍱 Swiggy/Zepto Pre-Orders

Students pre-order lunch or laser printouts from the lecture hall with scheduled pickup and 4-digit security OTP.

🍳 Real-Time Kitchen KDS

3-column Kanban order pipeline with second-by-second preparation timers and acoustic kitchen arrival chime.

Slide 4 • Security & Governance

Military-Grade Security & Anti-Fraud

Engineered to prevent screenshot replay attacks, counterfeit barcodes, and unauthorized spending.

⏱️ 30-Second Rolling HMAC Tokens

Barcodes rotate every 30 seconds. Screenshots and camera photos expire automatically, preventing theft.

🔒 Instant 1-Click Card Freeze

Lost ID cards can be locked in real time by students or administrators, instantly rejecting all POS taps.

📋 Immutable Audit Trail

Every wallet deduction, subsidy grant, and KDS handover is logged with IP address, terminal ID, and cryptographic cryptogram.
